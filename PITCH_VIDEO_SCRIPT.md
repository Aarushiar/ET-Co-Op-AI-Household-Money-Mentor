1. The problem.
2. The solution.
3. A start-to-finish demo of the agent workflow in the live app.

## One-Take Narration Script (Ready to Read)

"Household tax planning is usually fragmented. Couples miss deduction opportunities, struggle with old-vs-new regime choices, and spend too much time preparing declarations. ET Co-Op solves this with a single workflow: deterministic optimization, AI-guided explanation, and execution support.

This is the Joint Tax Terminal. For judges, we removed friction with one-click access. I click Try Live Demo, and we enter directly with prefilled dual-income data.

Now I run optimization. The board instantly shows tax leakage, current versus optimized tax, and a recommended regime. It also ranks tax-saving actions and shows advanced planning views like FIRE readiness and life-event impact.

Next, I generate AI Explanation plus HR Email Drafts. The system translates raw financial output into clear key moves and ready-to-use draft communication.

To complete the flow, I click Approve & Send to HR. We get immediate success confirmation. So from raw household inputs to guidance to action, the journey is continuous, fast, and deterministic.

Business impact is strong: lower manual advisory time, lower operational cost, and measurable recovery of leakage value for households. This is ET Co-Op: practical financial intelligence designed for decision speed."

## Recording Checklist

1. Record at 1080p, 30fps, system audio off unless needed.

3. Keep one tab only to avoid distractions.
4. Speak clearly, target 145 to 165 words per minute.
5. Keep total duration between 2:45 and 3:05.
